Publish & share
One-time setup
1. Your app lives in a GitHub repo as index.html.
2. Create an empty file data.json in the same repo (content: {}).

Every time you update
1. Tap Copy publish data.
2. Open data.json on GitHub → edit → paste → commit.
3. Done — players see it instantly.

Share link with players
Send them your app URL with ?view=1 on the end. They get read-only stats, no editing.

MVP race scoring
Points awarded per match, summed across all games. Tuned so attackers, midfielders and defenders can each top the leaderboard.
+3 if your team won
+1 for a draw
+2 per goal scored
+1.5 per assist
+4 per MVP award (any type)
−1 per fatigue flag
Defender bonus (registered DEF)
+3 if your team conceded ≤5 goals
+5 if your team conceded ≤3 goals
Midfielder bonus (registered MID)
+1 if your team conceded ≤5 goals
+3 if your team conceded ≤3 goals
Higher tier replaces the lower (non-stacking). Forwards don't earn defensive bonuses — their points come from goals and MVPs.
